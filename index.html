No. I  ·  Personal project  ·  2026

HydraXAI France

An explainable flood-risk map for every commune in mainland France.

Which communes are repeatedly declared flood disasters, and why? I joined official CatNat decrees with Météo-France soil moisture and daily rainfall (1982–2025) and IGN elevation data, then trained an XGBoost model validated by department, so it is always tested on territory it has never seen. Probabilities are calibrated, and every prediction is explained with SHAP, commune by commune, in a public Streamlit app.

Python · BigQuery · GeoPandas · XGBoost · SHAP · Streamlit

About

Same rigour, new material

For more than thirteen years I worked as a geological and geotechnical engineer in France and Algeria: contaminated sites and soils, environmental risk assessments, GIS mapping and seismic hazard studies, first in industry and then as an independent consultant.

That work was already about data: reading risk from incomplete measurements, mapping it, and explaining it to people who must make a decision. I completed Le Wagon's intensive data programme, a French state-certified qualification in AI and big data (level 6), to do this with modern tools.

Today I build end-to-end projects on geospatial and environmental data, and work as a freelance data analyst. I am looking for Data Scientist and Data Analyst roles, ideally on climate, energy or natural risk, in France or abroad.
